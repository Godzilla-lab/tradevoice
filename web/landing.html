The old way

Every trader keeps a second market in their head.

Who owes you. How much. Since when. You carry it all, and you carry it well, until the day you can’t.

Notebooks get wet. Pages get torn. A customer says, “Ah, I thought I paid.” And on the evening you want a loan, the bank asks the one thing paper cannot answer: show me your records.

TradeVoice is the notebook that listens.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to sign up?

Briefly. A WhatsApp number, a 6-digit code and a password you choose. No email, no card, no long forms. It takes about a minute.

What if I forget my password?

Reset it in a minute with a code on WhatsApp or email. When you do, we log you out of your other phones.

Can I change my number or business details?

Any time, in your profile. Changing your number needs a fresh code, so nobody else can take over your book.

Will it message my customers?

Never on its own. TradeVoice prepares reminders and receipts as drafts, and you press send.

What if it hears me wrong?

You see exactly what it understood before anything is saved. The parts it is unsure about are highlighted, so you change only those.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for recording. Entries are kept on your phone and sent when you are back online. A small Offline label tells you what is happening.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Pidgin, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo, by voice, by text, or from a photo of your notebook.

Can I lock it?

Yes. Choose your own PIN, hide amounts on screen, and if you forget the PIN you can get back in through WhatsApp.
