## Sound
- **Market ambience (credit needed):** "AMBTraf Ambience market hawker traffic near road BY SKY SOUND LIBRARY.wav" by
  SFXAFRIK (https://freesound.org/s/582962/), licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- Lagos bus stop ambience: "Ambient_SaboYaba_BusStop_Lagos_Nigeria.wav" by jehoshaphatia
  (https://freesound.org/s/411123/), CC0. No credit needed.
- Music: original, made in code for this film (`film/sound.py`). No licence needed.
- Product sounds (taps, ticks, sheets, the save chime): made in code (`film/sound.py`).
- Voices: Spitch text-to-speech (spitch.app). The voices are lucy (English), sade (Yorùbá), amina (Hausa) and ngozi
  (Igbo). The words are TradeVoice's own reply sentences (`src/tts.py`). The live app speaks with Intron.

## Picture
- Every phone screen is the real TradeVoice app, captured by `film/capture.cjs` with made-up names (Kemi Adebayo,
  Iya Bisi, Mama Ngozi, Oga Emeka, Alhaji Musa, Madam Funke). No real trader, customer or phone number.
- Fonts: Inter and Caveat, both under the SIL Open Font License (from Fontsource).
- Motion: GSAP 3.15 (GreenSock's standard no-charge licence).
- The phone is a plain, generic design. It is not any brand's device.
