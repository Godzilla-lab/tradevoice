## Part 1: Tax (the new laws)

### The basics
- Four laws signed **26 Jun 2025**, in force **1 Jan 2026**: Nigeria Tax Act (NTA), Nigeria Tax Administration Act
  (NTAA), Nigeria Revenue Service Act, Joint Revenue Board Act. They replace PITA, CITA, the VAT Act and others.
  ([Channels](https://www.channelstv.com/2025/06/26/breaking-tinubu-signs-four-tax-bills-into-law/),
  [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/ng/en/publications/the-nigerian-tax-reform-acts.html))
- **FIRS is now NRS** (Nigeria Revenue Service). Old TINs still work.
  ([Guardian](https://guardian.ng/business-services/firs-transitions-to-nigeria-revenue-service-as-new-tax-regime-begins/))
- A market trader working alone pays **personal income tax to their STATE revenue service** (e.g. LIRS), not NRS.
  ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nigeria/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))

### Personal income tax (on PROFIT, not sales)
| Taxable income per year | Rate |
|---|---|
| First ₦800,000 | **0%** |
| Next ₦2.2m | 15% |
| Next ₦9m | 18% |
| Next ₦13m | 21% |
| Next ₦25m | 23% |
| Above ₦50m | 25% |
([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/capital-gains-tax/1726922/understanding-personal-income-tax-under-the-nigerian-tax-act-2025),
[BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/individuals-earning-up-to-n100000-monthly-wont-pay-personal-income-tax-from-2026-oyedele/))
- Taxable income = income (business **profit**) minus reliefs. ₦800k is **not** sales.
- Reliefs (need proof): **rent relief 20% of annual rent, max ₦500k**; pension, NHF, NHIS, life insurance.
  ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-168.html)) → **rent receipts matter**.
- Filing deadline **31 March** (some states extend). Whether very small traders must file is [unverified].

### Informal traders: presumptive tax (2026 regulations)
- For businesses **without proper records**: tax presumed at about **1% of turnover** (sales).
- **"Nano" businesses exempt**: no fixed shop, no formal staff, turnover **≤ ₦12m** → free Tax Exemption Certificate.
  [partly unverified: one early report said ₦2m]
- **Cash and roadblock tax collection is banned.**
  ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/business-economy/article/explainer-presumptive-tax-how-nigeria-is-taxing-its-informal-economy/),
  [Andersen](https://ng.andersen.com/understanding-nigerias-presumptive-tax-regime-2026-structure-application-opportunities-and-risks/))
- 💡 **Our angle:** with records, a trader can be taxed on real profit instead of a presumed figure. Example (not
  advice): ₦6m sales at 1% = ₦60,000 presumed. If real profit is ₦700k, profit-based tax could be ₦0.
  **Records can save money**, but only a tax officer or professional can say which applies.

### Tax ID, penalties, records
- **NIN = Tax ID** for individuals; retrieve at the NRS Tax ID portal, reportedly **free**
  ([Channels](https://www.channelstv.com/2025/12/23/nin-now-automatic-tax-id-firs/)); portal address [unverified].
  Banks must ask **taxable persons** (people earning income) for it.
  ([Premium Times](https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/834807-explainer-the-new-tax-acts-and-tax-id-what-you-need-to-know.html))
- Penalties: not registering ₦50k then ₦25k/month; late filing ₦100k then ₦50k/month.
  ([Banwo & Ighodalo](https://www.banwo-ighodalo.com/grey-matter/nigerias-new-tax-regime-a-taxpayers-guide-to-compliance-offences-penalties-enforcement-and-dispute-resolution/))
- **Keep records 6 years** (electronic records count).
  ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/opinion/article/nigeria-tax-record-retention-know-the-rules/))
- Illegal levies / harassment → **Tax Ombud**, free: [taxombud.gov.ng](https://taxombud.gov.ng/).

### Money charges
- **₦50 stamp duty on transfers of ₦10,000+, paid by the SENDER** (not the trader receiving).
  ([Leadership](https://leadership.ng/tax-laws-banks-shift-stamp-duty-costs-on-electronic-transfers-to-senders/))
- VAT 7.5%; **basic food (rice, beans, yam…) zero-rated**; small businesses reportedly don't charge VAT
  (threshold [unverified], under review). Small company (incorporated) 0% CIT: ₦100m vs ₦50m turnover is disputed.

### Myths to bust (official answers)
| Myth | Official answer |
|---|---|
| "Govt will debit tax from my bank account" | "Nobody will debit your bank account": Oyedele ([Channels](https://www.channelstv.com/2025/12/30/tax-laws-govt-wont-debit-your-account-oyedele-assures-nigerians/)) |
| "Every transfer is taxed / the narration decides" | Tax is on declared income; narration is irrelevant ([Dubawa](https://dubawa.org/nigerias-tax-laws-where-does-bank-transfer-narration-stand/)) |
| "No Tax ID = no bank account for anyone" | Only people earning taxable income need it; NIN is the Tax ID |
| "₦800k means sales" | It means taxable **profit** after reliefs |
Some traders are moving back to cash out of fear, and that loses them the records lenders want.

### ✅ What TradeVoice MAY say (each with its source link in the app)
1. New tax laws started 1 Jan 2026; FIRS is now NRS.
2. If you trade alone, your income tax goes to your **state** revenue service.
3. Tax is on **profit**, not on everything you sell. Your records show your real profit.
4. The first ₦800,000 of taxable profit in a year is taxed at 0%.
5. Nobody can take tax from your account because of transfers; the narration doesn't create tax.
6. Your NIN is your Tax ID. It's free: don't pay anyone for it.
7. Cash and roadblock tax collection is banned: ask for an official receipt.
8. Think a levy is illegal? Complain free to the Tax Ombud (taxombud.gov.ng).
9. Keep your records 6 years. Keep rent receipts (rent relief).
10. The ₦50 transfer charge is paid by the sender.
11. **The rules are being reviewed now. Check with your state revenue office or a tax adviser before you pay.**

### ❌ What TradeVoice must NEVER say
Any exact tax bill · "you owe nothing" / "you are exempt" / "you are nano" for a specific trader · VAT thresholds ·
whether a specific levy is illegal (varies by state) · whether they must file · which expenses are deductible ·
advice to use cash or word transfers to avoid tax · any amnesty or waiver.

## Part 2: Loans, rent, levies, savings
(Same caveat: mostly from search summaries. Check numbers before they go on a slide.)

### Kredete (the prize sponsor)
Kredete sends money to Africa and **builds credit history** for African immigrants who have none in the US.
$22M Series A (AfricInvest, Partech), about 700k monthly users, average +58 US credit-score points; plans **rent
reporting**, credit-linked savings, goal-based loans
([Partech](https://partechpartners.com/news/kredete-raises-a-22m-series-a-round-to-expand-credit-building-infrastructure-with-stablecoin-transfers-to-africa),
[Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/kredete/)).
➡️ **Pitch line:** *"Kredete turns rent and payments into credit abroad; TradeVoice turns a market trader's daily
voice notes into the record a lender can trust at home."*

### Credit
- Lenders ask for BVN/NIN, **6 months of statements**, proof of trade, and a **shop rent receipt** (Moniepoint).
  Moniepoint sets loan limits from your Moniepoint transactions ([NairaCompare](https://nairacompare.ng/blogs/how-to-get-a-nirsal-microfinance-bank-loan-in-nigeria-2026),
  [naijaloanapp](https://naijaloanapp.com.ng/how-to-qualify-for-a-moniepoint-loan/)) [unverified].
- Loan apps charge **2.5–30% per MONTH** (PalmPay Flexi 0.6–1.5% per DAY); CBN policy rate 23% per YEAR
  ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/11/20/best-interest-rate-loan-apps-nigeria-2025/),
  [PM News](https://pmnewsnigeria.com/2026/09/22/cbn-cuts-interest-rate-to-23-from-26-5/)).
- Cash-flow lending works: a World Bank pilot saw **~99% repayment without collateral**
  ([World Bank](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/369c8618-a46a-44e6-9162-a6b556e5255f/content)).
  Lenders trust **verifiable** records (transfers/POS) more than self-typed ones → label entries
  "transfer (ref …)" vs "cash (self-reported)".
- Government: TraderMoni ₦10k–50k at 0% + 2.5% fee; PCGS ₦50k grant for nano businesses; DBN lends via 84 partners
  (81% of 2025 end-borrowers women). ⚠️ Many "GEEP 2026 portal" sites are fake.
- Build a credit file: borrow small and repay on time with a lender that reports to **CRC / FirstCentral /
  CreditRegistry**. Banks can recover a defaulted loan from **any account linked to your BVN** (GSI).

### Predatory loans (FCCPC DEON rules, upheld by court 20 Jul 2026)
Loan apps may **not** contact your friends/family/employer or shame you; they must show all fees upfront; fines up to
₦100M. About **505 approved lenders**; check the FCCPC list before borrowing
([TheCable](https://www.thecable.ng/fccpc-resumes-enforcement-as-court-upholds-digital-lending-regulations-limits-licensing-to-ncc/),
[FCCPC list](https://fccpc.gov.ng/registration-of-digital-money-lenders/approvals-of-dmls/)). No interest cap found.

### Rent: the big shock
Shops cost ₦500k to ₦5M+/yr, plus "attachment" and agent fees (Balogun). Ibadan Gbagi rents jumped from ₦20–30k to ₦1.6–7.5M.
**Lagos Tenancy Law 2011:** for a sitting yearly tenant, a landlord may not demand **more than 1 year** in
advance (some areas excluded)
([NPC](https://nigeriapropertycentre.com/blog/renting-letting/the-lagos-tenancy-law-in-detail)).
**42% of informal businesses can't survive one month without income**
([Champion/Moniepoint](https://championnews.com.ng/2025/10/19/79-of-informal-businesses-record-increase-in-cost-moniepoint-report/)).

### Levies & touts
₦400–1,800 **per day** to touts/levies in Lagos markets; one trader paid ~₦576k in a year; tickets marked ₦200
while ₦2,000 is collected
([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/businessday-investigation/article/in-nigerias-largest-city-smes-choke-as-touts-levy-multiple-taxes/)).
Lagos is merging 60+ levies into one daily market ticket; unauthorised collection is punishable by up to ₦5M / 3 yrs.
A legal levy has an **official state/LGA receipt**.

### Safety nets, payments, inflation
- Fire: ≥25 major market fires Jan–May 2025; only **8% of MSMEs insured**; about 85% of Nigerians lack health insurance.
- Micro-pension reached only about 200k people; PenCom is launching a digital Personal Pension Plan.
- Fees: transfers ₦10–50; **₦50 stamp duty paid by the sender** (≥₦10k); POS ₦100 per ₦20k plus the agent's fee.
- **Fake transfer alerts:** only the balance counts, not the SMS or screenshot.
- Inflation Aug 2026: **15.39%, food 19.57%**. 65% of informal firms saw revenue rise but only 47% saw profit rise.
  **Real profit = sales − cost to restock TODAY − rent/levy set-aside − the owner's own pay.**
- **Kippa (about 500k users) went dark in Jan 2024 and users lost their books.** Traders must be able to export their data.
