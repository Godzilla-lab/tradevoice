# Tax in TradeVoice

**Where:** Profile → "Tax and your records". Chat, WhatsApp and Ask also answer "Do I pay tax?" / "Ṣé mo máa san owó orí?" / "zan biya haraji?".

**What it shows**
- The trader's own year from the book: sales, spending, sales minus spending, where the money went, rent and levy payments (keep the receipts: rent relief).
- 9 plain facts about the 2026 tax laws, each with its source link (text in `tax.py`, 5 languages).
- A "Year record for the tax office" download (the same statement the lender gets, with the year tables).
- Always: "The rules are being reviewed now. Check with your state revenue service or a tax adviser before you pay. This is not tax advice."

**What it never does:** say how much tax someone owes or whether they must pay. The AI may repeat the facts only (assistant.py prompt) and every number it says must be in the book or the facts.

**Why it matters (pitch):** without records a trader can be taxed on a guess from sales; with records they can show real profit, and the first ₦800,000 of taxable profit a year is at 0%. Your book protects you.

**Before real traders see it:** a Nigerian tax professional checks the facts, native speakers check the Yoruba / Hausa / Igbo / Pidgin. Research and sources: [FINANCE_RESEARCH.md](FINANCE_RESEARCH.md).
